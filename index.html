How to play

You wake in a bed, and the power is out. You have five days. Every time he catches you, you wake up on the next day: the halls are rebuilt and he gets faster. Get caught on day five and it is over. The exit is sealed. Search the rooms off the halls for 4 fuses glowing on pedestals, then open the exit and walk into the light.

Close doors behind you. A shut door hides you from his eyes, and an open one you did not open means he has already been through.

Your flashlight runs on batteries. Take bottles and throw one: he will go and look. Pick items up with E and drop them with Q; the item in your hand is the one you drop (mouse wheel or 1-3 to switch). A brass key opens the room he never visits; spare batteries are used with R. Hold C or CTRL to crouch: you move slowly, but quietly and lower. Notes tell you what happened here.

Lockers are your only shelter. If he sees you climb in, he will drag you out. Sprinting is loud. Lights fail. Don't stop moving.
